  • Noisy Neighbor CPU Starvation
    Shared hosting places hundreds of sites on one machine. A traffic spike on another account directly degrades your TTFB and PHP execution time.
  • PHP Worker Queuing & Bottlenecks
    Limited PHP workers mean requests queue up. Under real traffic, your server responds in seconds, not milliseconds—Lighthouse scores crater.
  • No Intelligent Caching Architecture
    Generic hosting lacks Redis object caching, server-level full-page caching, and edge CDN. Every request hits the origin—every single time.
  • Database Bottlenecks at Traffic Peaks
    Shared MySQL on spinning disks can't handle concurrent query loads. WooCommerce and high-traffic blogs get unpredictable query times during peak hours.
  • Zero Scalability on Traffic Events
    Product launches, media mentions, email blasts—your shared host collapses. Kinsta auto-scales. You capture the spike instead of crashing through it.
  • Shared SSL & Security Exposure
    Shared IPs mean your domain reputation is tied to every other site on the server. A blacklisted neighbor can trigger email filtering and SEO penalties on your domain.
